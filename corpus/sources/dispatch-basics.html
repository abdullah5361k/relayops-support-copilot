Dispatch basics

Triage a new job

Start every new job by confirming the service address, contact method, access constraints, requested work, and preferred appointment window. Dispatchers classify urgency from observable impact rather than guessing a diagnosis. Save concise notes that a technician can verify on arrival.

Assign a technician

Choose an active technician whose specialty matches the work and whose schedule has enough travel time. If no suitable person is available, offer the next documented window and explain that scheduling is provisional until the customer confirms it.

Closeout checklist

After a visit, verify the job status, work summary, parts notes, and customer follow-up preference. A completed status must reflect an actual field update; dispatchers do not mark a job complete merely because its planned time has passed.
