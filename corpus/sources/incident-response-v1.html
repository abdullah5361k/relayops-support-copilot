Incident response policy

Acknowledgement

For a service interruption reported through RelayOps, the on-call coordinator acknowledges a confirmed urgent report within four business hours. This baseline policy was published for the fictional beta service and is retained only as a historical source version.

Updates

The coordinator records the incident reference, impact, owner, and next update time. Do not promise a repair time before an owner has assessed the report. Customers receive concise factual updates through their agreed support channel.
