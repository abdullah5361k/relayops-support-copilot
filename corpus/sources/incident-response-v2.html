Incident response policy

Acknowledgement

For a confirmed urgent service interruption, the RelayOps on-call coordinator acknowledges the report within one business hour. This replaces the earlier four-business-hour beta baseline. Acknowledgement means ownership and a next-update time, not a promise that work is complete.

Updates

The coordinator records the incident reference, impact, owner, and next update time. Send factual progress updates every two business hours until mitigation, then record the resolution and follow-up owner in the incident log.
